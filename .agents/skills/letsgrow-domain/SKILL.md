# LetsGrow Domain

## Use This First

LetsGrow is Danish-first recurring meeting improvement software for team leads, managers, scrum masters, and other meeting owners.

The launch wedge is narrow:

- improve recurring meetings, not feedback in general
- show whether a meeting gets better over time
- collect lightweight post-meeting feedback in the workflow teams already use
- keep participants account-free
- optimize for first real feedback on a real recurring meeting

Challenge work that drifts into HR, broad surveys, generic productivity, personal growth, or "feedback on anything."

## Strategic Filter

Before proposing a feature, flow, screen, campaign, or copy change, check whether it:

1. Helps a meeting owner improve a recurring meeting.
2. Reduces friction around collecting feedback after meetings.
3. Makes patterns over time clearer.
4. Keeps participants account-free and low effort.
5. Fits a Danish-first, small-to-midsize B2B launch.
6. Avoids sounding like an HR platform, survey tool, or broad self-improvement app.

If the answer is no, challenge the work and propose a tighter recurring-meeting version.

## Defaults

- Market: Denmark first.
- Customer-facing language: plain Danish unless asked otherwise.
- Primary CTA: `Få tidlig adgang`.
- Onboarding: founder-assisted or manual workspace setup during pilot.
- Account model: one workspace per company or team.
- First user: workspace owner, usually also the first meeting owner.
- Participants: answer feedback without accounts.
- Activation: one real recurring meeting configured and first participant responses received.
- Pricing validation: keep pricing semi-private; test 499 DKK/month for one meeting owner and 299 DKK/month per extra meeting owner.
