# Messaging And Commercial Guidance

Use this for website copy, CTAs, Danish messaging, pricing, and commercial model decisions.

## Language And Tone

Customer-facing launch website, marketing copy, and most UX copy should be Danish unless the user asks otherwise.

Tone:

- simple
- concrete
- calm
- useful
- confident without hype

Avoid American SaaS cliches, broad culture claims, generic "unlock growth" language, and `AI-powered` as the launch message.

## Approved Copy Directions

Use or adapt:

- `Gør jeres møder bedre, uge for uge`
- `Få enkel feedback efter hvert møde og se, om kvaliteten stiger`
- `Bedre teammøder starter med en lille vane`
- `Se om jeres ugentlige møder faktisk bliver bedre`
- `Mindre mavefornemmelse. Mere synlig udvikling`
- `Inviter feedback@letsgrow.dk og kom i gang uden tung opsætning`
- `Ingen lange spørgeskemaer. Kun få spørgsmål efter mødet`
- `Få et tydeligt billede af, om kvaliteten stiger, står stille eller falder`

## Homepage Narrative

Recommended structure:

1. Hero: who it is for, what improves, and why it matters.
2. Problem: recurring meetings consume time but rarely improve systematically.
3. How it works: create meeting, invite/connect LetsGrow, collect short feedback, track quality.
4. Why recurring meetings: repetition makes improvement measurable.
5. Outcomes: better clarity, energy, usefulness, and follow-through.
6. FAQ: workflow change, survey length, company size, best meeting types.
7. CTA: early access.

Hero must make recurring meetings obvious and speak to team leads or meeting owners.

## CTA

Default primary CTA:

- `Få tidlig adgang`

Default destination:

- Typeform or equivalent early-access intake

Secondary CTA:

- `Se hvordan det virker`

Avoid `Book demo`, `Talk to sales`, app-store download, or public checkout as the primary launch CTA unless the operating model changes.

## Commercial Model

Sell:

- better recurring meetings
- less wasted meeting time
- visible improvement over time
- a lightweight habit for team leads

Do not sell "more survey responses."

Expansion path:

1. Land with one meeting owner.
2. Prove value on a small number of recurring meetings.
3. Add more meeting owners inside the workspace.
4. Later expand to departments or larger company usage.

## Pricing

Launch with owner-based pricing inside one workspace subscription.

Avoid participant-based pricing because it makes cost unpredictable and discourages inviting the right people.

Suggested validation phases:

- Design partner: free for 6-8 weeks with active feedback calls.
- Early access paid: 499 DKK/month per workspace, includes 1 meeting owner, up to 3 recurring meetings, and up to 25 participants total.
- Extra meeting owner: 299 DKK/month.
- Later self-serve: Starter 499 DKK/month, Team 1,499 DKK/month, Business custom.

Keep pricing semi-private until the offer is validated. Do not add a full public pricing table too early.

## Payment Timing

Do not ask for card details before the team sees first real value.

Early default:

- free pilot or founder-led setup
- no credit card upfront
- no self-serve checkout
- manual invoicing only if a paid pilot is needed

Later:

- 14-day free trial
- no credit card upfront
- workspace owner starts subscription
- extra meeting owners adjust the workspace subscription
