# Audience And Positioning

Use this for ICP, market, JTBD, positioning, and prioritization decisions.

## Category

Recurring meeting improvement software.

LetsGrow is not a general feedback platform, survey builder, HR suite, productivity dashboard, or personal development app.

## Launch Audience

Primary buyer:

- Danish team lead
- manager
- scrum master
- department or project lead
- anyone visibly responsible for recurring meeting quality

Best-fit organizations:

- small to midsize knowledge-work companies
- teams with recurring meetings already in place
- short buying cycles and limited procurement friction
- one motivated meeting owner who can start small

Best-fit meeting types:

- weekly team meetings
- 1:1s
- sprint reviews
- leadership check-ins
- recurring project or operating meetings

## Buyer Motivation

The buyer wants less wasted meeting time and a clearer signal about whether meetings are useful, clear, energizing, and followed through.

The emotional context is practical frustration, not inspiration. The buyer usually knows a meeting could be better but lacks a lightweight way to measure improvement.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When my team meeting repeats every week, I want to know whether people find it useful so I can improve the format instead of relying on gut feel.
- When I run a recurring meeting, I want feedback to happen automatically after the meeting so I do not have to chase people.
- When I change a meeting format, I want to see whether quality improves over the next cycles.
- When I invite participants, I want them to answer quickly without account creation or a long survey.

## Positioning Pillars

- Better recurring meetings: meetings that repeat should improve over time.
- Feedback with low friction: feedback must fit the workflow teams already use.
- Visible improvement over time: repeated signals matter more than one-off opinions.

## Decision Rule

If a feature or message would also fit Typeform, Culture Amp, an HR engagement suite, or a generic productivity app, narrow it until it clearly serves recurring meeting improvement for meeting owners.
