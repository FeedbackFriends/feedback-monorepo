# iOS domain and product language

Paths are relative to `ios-app/` unless stated otherwise.

## Domain And Product Language

- LetsGrow is Danish-first and focused on honest feedback after meetings, workshops, talks, training, and similar repeatable formats.
- Participants answer feedback without accounts.
- Owners plan in their existing calendar and invite a generated per-format LetsGrow email address.
- Preserve the code/API distinction between activity/format, event/session, and participant-event concepts. Check existing models and visible copy before naming new types or screens.

## Localization migration

For the iOS localization migration, follow the [localization conventions](../../../docs/ios-localization-conventions.md) and update the [source inventory](../../../docs/ios-localization-inventory.md). They define the dedicated Localization module, Danish terminology, semantic keys, typed parameters, plural/formatting policy, and app-owned versus external text boundaries. Implementation progress lives in the [phase checklist](../../../docs/localization-phases.md).
